Haplina Stream Pack Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Haplina Stream Pack accesses, why it accesses it, where that information is stored, and how users can disconnect services or remove locally stored data.

Effective: 9 September 2026 Contact: David@c0degeek.dev

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Haplina Stream Pack, a desktop/local companion and streaming toolkit for OBS Studio. Haplina Stream Pack can connect to third-party streaming services, including YouTube and Twitch, when a user chooses to enable those integrations.

2. Local-first design

Haplina Stream Pack is designed to run on the user's own computer. The application does not require a Haplina account and is not designed around a Haplina-hosted cloud service. Configuration and connection data used by the local application are stored on the user's device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

3. Google and YouTube data Haplina may access

If a user chooses to connect a Google/YouTube account, Haplina Stream Pack uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. The exact data available depends on the OAuth scopes the user is shown and grants. Haplina may use authorized YouTube API access to provide streaming-related features such as:

  • identifying the authorized YouTube channel and related channel metadata;
  • finding the user's current or scheduled live broadcast and associated live-chat identifier;
  • reading live-chat messages and related metadata needed to display a combined or local chat view;
  • sending a live-chat message when the user explicitly submits one through Haplina;
  • performing a supported chat moderation or live-broadcast action when the user explicitly invokes that feature and the granted scope permits it.

Haplina Stream Pack does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts or unrelated Google account information for these features.

4. How Google/YouTube data is used

Google and YouTube data is used only to provide the streaming feature the user selected, such as identifying a live broadcast, displaying chat, sending a requested chat message, or carrying out another user-initiated YouTube action supported by the application. Haplina does not use Google user data for advertising, credit scoring, surveillance, sale to data brokers, or unrelated profiling.

Haplina Stream Pack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. OAuth credentials and tokens

When a user authorizes YouTube access, OAuth access and refresh tokens may be stored locally so Haplina can reconnect without requiring a new sign-in every time. These tokens are authentication credentials and should be treated as sensitive. They are used only to authenticate requests to the corresponding service on behalf of the user.

Haplina does not intentionally publish OAuth tokens, stream-service tokens or other private connection credentials as part of its public website or distributable source files.

6. Twitch, StreamElements and other connected services

Haplina may also connect to other services selected by the user, such as Twitch or StreamElements. Depending on the service and feature, Haplina may access channel identifiers, chat or event data, account/channel metadata, and authentication tokens required to provide the requested integration. Information obtained from those services is used for the related streaming feature and is also subject to the terms and privacy policies of those services.

7. Storage and retention

Haplina stores local configuration for as long as the user keeps it on the device. Saved connection credentials may remain locally until the user disconnects or forgets the connection, deletes Haplina's local data, or uninstalls/removes the application and its data files. Temporary information used while a stream is running, such as transient chat state or an in-memory alert queue, may be discarded when the application is stopped unless a feature explicitly saves it.

8. Sharing and disclosure

Haplina does not sell Google/YouTube user data. It does not intentionally share Google/YouTube user data with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties. Data is transmitted to the third-party service itself when necessary to perform an authorized API request, for example sending a YouTube chat message to YouTube.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the software and its users.

9. Revoking access

A user can stop Haplina from using a connected service by disconnecting or forgetting that connection in Haplina where that control is available, and by revoking the application's access in the service provider's account settings.

For Google accounts, users can review and revoke third-party access from their Google Account's security and connections settings. Revoking access prevents Haplina from using the revoked Google authorization unless the user authorizes it again.

10. Deleting locally stored data

Users can remove locally stored Haplina configuration and credentials by using the application's forget/disconnect controls where available or by deleting the application's local data files after stopping Haplina. Removing local data does not itself delete information already held by YouTube, Twitch, StreamElements or another third-party service; those services control their own stored data.

11. Security

Haplina is designed to keep its local control interface on the user's machine and to avoid exposing private credential files as public web content. Nevertheless, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their operating system account secure, avoid sharing credential files, and revoke access promptly if they believe a token or account has been compromised.

12. Children's privacy

Haplina Stream Pack is intended for streamers who are able to authorize the connected services they use. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a Haplina-operated online service.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Haplina's features, data handling or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the effective date.

14. Contact

Privacy questions about Haplina Stream Pack can be sent to David@c0degeek.dev.
